Baseline adjustment accounts for activity numbers (admission or attendance) that may be under- or over-counted at the hospital trust.
It relates to Hospital Episodes Statistics (HES) data (from providers) via Secondary Users Service (SUS).

The adjustment required is the percentage adjustment to the baseline data currently extracted for your trust, broken down by point-of-delivery (POD) and treatment specialty.
Note that categories with a baseline count of fewer than 10 are grouped into an 'Other' category.

Click the 'Download Baseline Values (CSV)' button below to download the data for further inspection.
You can open this file in programs like Microsoft Excel among others.

Switch between activity types and PODs using the tabs in the 'Baseline Counts' box and move the slider next to a specialty to make an adjustment.

Please provide the reason for your adjustments in the 'Supporting Rationale' box and cite an evidence base.
