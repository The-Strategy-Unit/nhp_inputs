This tool is designed to help facilitate the use of the OpenPlan Hospital Demand Model demand to support the development of robust, local proprosals for the New Hospital Programme (NHP).

The NHP requires estimates of future activity levels to inform the design of a new hospital.
This tool is designed to help determine how hospital activity might change in the years to come (relative to a baseline year) and to provide a high-level view of the physical capacity required to meet that demand.

For more information or help, please see the [OpenPlan demand model documentation](https://connect.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/nhp/project_information/), or contact the [MLCSU NHP Analytics mailbox](mailto:mlcsu.nhpanalytics@nhs.net).

When communicating about a particular scenario, please quote the information in the 'Scenario Options' table on this page.
